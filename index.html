쓰는 목적

대국 상대를 짝짓고 판을 진행하는 것, 급수와 순위를 셈하는 것, 주간 대회를 운영하는 것, 엔진 사용·비매너 같은 신고를 살피고 제재하는 것, 무료 이용 한도를 지키는 것에만 씁니다. 광고·마케팅이나 프로필 분석에는 쓰지 않으며, 팔거나 빌려주지 않습니다.

다른 이용자에게 보이는 것

닉네임, 프로필 아이콘, 급수, 레이팅, 승패 기록, 들어간 문파는 대국 상대와 순위표 · 친구 · 문파 화면에 보입니다. 랭크 대국 · 주간 대회 · 리그 대국은 다른 이용자가 관전할 수 있고(두는 중에는 두 수 늦게), 끝난 판의 수순도 볼 수 있습니다. 친구 대국은 두 사람만 볼 수 있습니다. 대화 글은 그 대화에 든 사람(두 대국자 · 같은 문파원)만 봅니다.

보관 기간과 지우기

서버의 정보는 계정을 지울 때까지 보관합니다. 앱의 설정 › 계정 삭제로 언제든 지울 수 있고, 지우면 프로필·대국 기록·이용 기록·신고와 차단 기록에서 이 계정의 정보를 지체 없이 파기합니다. 상대의 기록에 남은 판에서는 이 계정이 드러나지 않게 합니다. 결제 기록은 같은 거래로 두 번 받지 않도록 계정과의 연결만 끊고 남깁니다.

절대장기 (Jeoldae Janggi) — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-10-22

For online games against other people, the app keeps only what the service needs on our server. We never ask for your name, email, or phone number. Games against the AI and puzzles still run entirely on your device, and their records never leave it.

What is stored on the server when you play online

Anonymous accountA random identifier and sign-in token created the first time you use an online feature. It is not linked to any email, phone number, or social account.
ProfileThe nickname you choose; rank, promotion points, and rating; wins, losses, and streaks; weekly cup results and points; sign-up and last-seen times
Game recordsFor each online game: opponent, piece setup, moves, time spent and timestamp of each move, and the result
UsageHow many online games you played each day (used for the free daily limit), and matchmaking queue entries (deleted once the game ends or you stop searching)
Friends and clansYour friend list and requests, friend code and invites, the clan (문파) you joined and your role, your clan score contributions, and the profile icon you chose
ChatMessages and timestamps in game chats both players opened and in clan chats (profanity is masked before storage)
Cup and leagueWins, losses, points, and standings in the weekly cup and the league
WalletYour coin balance, the boards, pieces and profile icons you own, and a log of coins received and spent (check-ins, quests, purchases, game fees, free refills)
Device numberA random number the app creates in this device's keychain. It is not an advertising identifier; it is used only to limit the welcome coins, invite rewards and wallet transfer to once per device.
PurchasesApp Store transaction IDs, the product, purchase and (for subscriptions) expiry dates, and refund status. Payment details such as card numbers are handled by Apple; we never receive them.
Reports and blocksWho reported whom, the reason, any note you wrote, your block list, and any restrictions
Access logsOur hosting provider may keep access logs such as IP addresses for a short period for security and abuse prevention.

Why we use it

Only to match opponents and run games, calculate ranks and rankings, run the weekly cup, review and act on reports such as engine use or bad manners, and enforce the free daily limit. We do not use it for advertising, marketing, or profiling, and we do not sell or rent it.

What other players can see

Your nickname, rank, rating, and win/loss record are shown to opponents and on the rankings. Ranked and weekly cup games can be watched by other players, including their move lists after they end. Friend games are visible only to the two players.

Processors

Supabase Inc.Operates our server (database, sign-in, realtime delivery). Data is stored in the Seoul, South Korea region.

Retention and deletion

Server data is kept until you delete your account. You can do so at any time in Settings › Delete account; your profile, game records, usage, reports, and blocks are then destroyed without delay, and games in other players' records no longer identify you. Purchase records are kept with the account link removed, so the same transaction cannot be redeemed twice.

The anonymous sign-in is stored in this device's keychain, so reinstalling the app on the same device signs you back in to the same account (coins, cosmetics, rank). On a different or reset device you cannot sign back in to it; contact us and we will delete what remains on the server.

What stays on your device

The following is written only to the app's own storage. We cannot see it, and deleting the app removes it.

AI gamesAI ladder rating and record, game in progress, the last 20 games with per-move evaluations
PuzzlesWhich puzzles you solved and your records
SettingsSound and haptics, chosen opponent level, piece setup, and similar choices

A photo you choose for position analysis is read on the device and never uploaded. The app receives only the one photo you pick, not your photo library. We do not access location, contacts, camera, or microphone, we do not read advertising identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Device backup

App data may be included in Android auto-backup or in your iOS device backup (iCloud or computer). These are Google and Apple features and are not transmitted to us.

Children

Online play is intended for users aged 14 and over. If we learn that a child under 14 used online features without a legal guardian's consent, we delete that account's data. AI games and puzzles collect nothing and are available at any age.

Your rights

You may ask to access, correct, delete, or stop the processing of your data. You can change your nickname in the app and delete your account in Settings; send any other request to the contact below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this document and the date above. If a change adds any data collection, we will say so inside the app.
