보관 기간과 파기

서버의 정보는 계정을 지울 때까지 보관합니다. 앱의 설정 › 계정 삭제로 언제든 지울 수 있고, 지우면 프로필 · 소셜 로그인 정보 · 대국 기록 · 이용 기록 · 대화 · 신고와 차단 기록에서 이 계정의 정보를 지체 없이 파기합니다. 카카오 · Apple로 로그인한 계정은 그 서비스와의 연결도 함께 끊습니다. 상대의 기록에 남은 판에서는 이 계정이 드러나지 않게 합니다.

결제 기록(거래 번호 · 산 상품 · 산 때 · 끝나는 때 · 환불 여부)은 「전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률」에 따라 계정을 지운 뒤에도 계정과의 연결을 끊은 채 5년 동안 보관하고(같은 거래로 두 번 받지 않게 막는 데도 씁니다), 마지막으로 바뀐 때부터 5년이 지나면 서버가 자동으로 파기합니다.

기기 번호와 그 기기로 받은 보상 종류(환영 코인 · 초대 보상 · 지갑 옮기기)는 계정을 지운 뒤에도 계정과 잇지 않은 채 서비스를 끝낼 때까지 보관하며, 같은 기기에서 같은 보상을 다시 받지 못하게 막는 데만 씁니다. 이용이 제한된 계정이 쓴 기기 번호는 그 제한이 끝날 때까지 남겨, 계정을 지우거나 새 게스트로 들어와도 같은 기기에서는 제한이 이어지게 합니다.

파기는 데이터베이스에서 복구할 수 없게 지우는 방법으로 하며, 서버 백업에 남은 사본은 백업 보관 기간이 지나면 함께 사라집니다.

게스트 계정의 로그인 정보는 이 기기의 키체인과 앱 설정에 저장되므로, 앱을 지웠다 다시 깔거나 iCloud · 컴퓨터 백업이나 기기 간 옮기기로 새 기기에 옮겨도 같은 계정(코인 · 꾸미기 · 급수)으로 들어옵니다. 백업 없이 기기를 초기화하거나 새 기기를 쓰면 게스트 계정으로는 다시 들어올 수 없으니, 소셜 로그인을 이어 두시길 권합니다. 이때 서버에 남은 정보는 문의처로 요청하면 지웁니다.

절대장기 (Jeoldae Janggi) — Privacy Policy

Last updated 2026-11-01

For online games against other people, the app keeps only what the service needs on our server. If you start as a guest, we never ask for your name, email, or phone number. If you sign in with Kakao, Google, or Apple, we receive the information listed under "Social sign-in" from that service. Games against the AI run entirely on your device and their moves and records never leave it (only the result of the placement games that set your starting rank, and whether you completed AI quests, are stored).

What is stored on the server when you play online

AccountA random identifier and sign-in token created the first time you use an online feature. A guest account is not linked to any email, phone number, or social account.
Social sign-inWhat the service you choose sends us — Kakao: email, nickname, profile photo URL / Google: email, name, profile photo URL / Apple: email (a relay address if you choose “Hide My Email”) and name — plus your member number at that service. For Apple sign-in we keep an Apple refresh token so that we can revoke the link when you delete your account.
ProfileThe nickname and profile icon you choose; rank, promotion points, and rating; wins, losses, and streaks; cup and league results; sign-up and last-seen times
Game recordsFor each online game: opponent, piece setup, moves, time spent and timestamp of each move, and the result
UsageHow many online games you played each day (used for the daily play limit), matchmaking queue entries (deleted once the game ends or you stop searching), and records of leaving games early

Retention and deletion

Server data is kept until you delete your account. You can do so at any time in Settings › Delete account; your profile, social sign-in data, game records, usage, chat, reports, and blocks are then destroyed without delay, and the link to Kakao or Apple is revoked. Games in other players' records no longer identify you. Purchase records (transaction ID, product, purchase and expiry dates, refund status) are kept, unlinked from the account, for five years as required by Korean e-commerce law (they also stop the same transaction from being redeemed twice), and the server deletes them automatically five years after their last change. The device number and which device rewards it has received (welcome coins, invite rewards, wallet transfer) are kept, unlinked from any account, until the service ends, only to stop the same device from receiving them again. The device numbers used by a restricted account are kept until the restriction ends, so that deleting the account or starting over as a guest on the same device does not lift it. Deleted data is erased from the database and disappears from server backups when the backup retention period ends.

A guest sign-in is stored in this device's keychain and app settings, so reinstalling the app, or moving to a new device with an iCloud or computer backup or device-to-device transfer, signs you back in to the same account. On a device reset without a backup, a guest account cannot be recovered, so we recommend linking a social sign-in; contact us and we will delete what remains on the server.

Security

All traffic between the app and the server is encrypted (HTTPS). The server only lets each user read and write their own data (row-level security), and wallets, purchases and sign-in tokens can be handled only through server functions. We never collect passwords or payment card details.

What stays on your device

AI ladder rating and record, games in progress, recent game records and per-move evaluations, quest progress, and settings are written only to the app's own storage. We cannot see them, and deleting the app removes them. The app does not access location, contacts, camera, microphone, or your photo library, does not use cookies or advertising identifiers, and contains no advertising SDKs; the only analytics is what the purchase library (Unity IAP) sends to Unity when you make a purchase.

Device backup

App data may be included in Android auto-backup or in your iOS device backup (iCloud or computer). These are Google and Apple features and are not transmitted to us.
