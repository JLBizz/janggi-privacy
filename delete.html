남는 것과 기간

  • 결제 기록(거래 번호 · 산 상품 · 산 때 · 끝나는 때 · 환불 여부) — 계정과의 연결을 끊은 채 5년. 「전자상거래 등에서의 소비자보호에 관한 법률」에 따른 보관이에요.
  • 기기 번호와 그 기기로 받은 보상 종류 — 계정과 잇지 않은 채 서비스를 끝낼 때까지. 같은 기기에서 환영 코인 · 초대 보상을 다시 받지 못하게 막는 데만 써요. 이용이 제한된 계정의 기기 번호는 그 제한이 끝날 때까지 남아요.
  • 서버 백업에 남은 사본 — 백업 보관 기간(7일)이 지나면 함께 사라져요.

Delete your 절대장기 (Jeoldae Janggi) account

In the app

Open the app, go to Settings › Delete account, and confirm. The account is deleted immediately and cannot be restored; links to Kakao or Apple are revoked. A Plus subscription is not cancelled automatically — cancel it in the App Store or Google Play.

Without the app

Email leejunmo@kakao.com with the subject "Delete account" and your nickname or friend code. We confirm the account, delete it without delay, and reply.

What is deleted

Your nickname, profile icon, rank and record, the email, name and member number from social sign-in, online game records, usage, friends and clan, chat messages, reports and blocks, push token, coins, cosmetics and quest records.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records (transaction ID, product, dates, refund status) — unlinked from the account, for 5 years as required by Korean e-commerce law.
  • Device number and which device rewards it received — unlinked from any account, until the service ends, only to stop the same device from claiming them again. Device numbers of a restricted account are kept until the restriction ends.
  • Server backups — copies disappear when the backup retention period (7 days) ends.

See the privacy policy for details.
